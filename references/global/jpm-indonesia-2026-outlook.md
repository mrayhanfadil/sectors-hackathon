## 2. One-Paragraph Thesis (p1 verbatim distilled)

After a political-transition year 2025, 2026 is a pivotal growth-foundation year: higher government spending (fiscal budget + Danantara) drives consumption re-acceleration, backed by improving global macro, easing geopolitical tensions and a global liquidity easing cycle. Balanced risk-reward - Neutral on Indonesia per Asia/EM & ASEAN strategy. Execution risk (revenue shortfall, SAL depletion) and tight banking-system liquidity remain overhangs; rupiah volatility is the key downside risk.

## 6. Flows Narrative (retail vs institutional, Danantara Value-Up, MSCI free-float risk)

### 6.1 Retail - record resurgence, same as COVID peak

- **58% of ADTV** in Oct-25 (Figure 9) - highest since 2021.
- **IDR 14.5tn retail ADTV** Oct-25 - *record high*, surpasses prior COVID peak IDR 14.4tn Jan-21.
- Driver: low-free-float **conglomerate-related stocks** + **index speculation** on MSCI inclusion (JCI outpaced MXID/LQ45 by **16-22% YTD**; Figure 10).
- Quant funds alongside retail amplified the move (p1).
- **Outlook:** elevated in **1H26**, potential moderation in **2H26**.

### 6.2 MSCI Adjusted Free Float - the swing risk

- MSCI's new **Adjusted Free Float definition** likely **announced 1Q26, implemented May-2026** (p1, p8-9).
- Could deflate/spec-out the low-float conglomerate rally; JPM flags as the key variable for whether retail participation moderates.
- Conglomerate surge = performance divergence (bars: MXID -3.6% YTD USD vs JCI +16-22% ahead).
- Our template implication: need an **Index/Regulatory risk bucket** explicitly for MSCI inclusion/exclusion/free-float review.

### 6.3 Foreign & Institutional - historic capitulation, poised to reverse

- **YTD foreign equity outflows ~US$2.2bn** (Figure 4, adj. for one-off crossings); **~US$2.6bn over 2 years** (p23).
- **FDI -28% YoY** (JPM Economics, 9M25), **FPI -US$14bn in 9M25** (equities + bonds) (Table 9, p21).
- **SRBI non-resident ownership down materially YTD** (Figure 43); foreign capital moving overseas instead of gov't bonds (Figure 44).
- **Foreign ownership 44%** (KSEI scripless, 30 Sep 2025) - levels last seen May-24 (Figure 48).
- **EM investor survey net underweight Indonesia highest since 2003** (Figure 47) - positioning washed out.
- **Outlook:** JPM expects outflows to subside → strong reversal on: growth re-acceleration, policy reform clarity (PP 28/2025 deregulation, political stability), global easing cycle, low debt/GDP ~40% fiscal headroom. **Beneficiaries:** Financials, Telcos, big caps / index heavyweights; also improves system liquidity → second-order to Consumer Discretionary, Autos, Property.

### 6.4 Domestic Institutional - the incoming bid

- **Danantara public investment mandate** - speculated **~US$1.5bn (~2% of IDX mcap)** deployment into domestic capital market ([CNBC Indonesia 5 Nov 2025 - link p28-29](https://www.cnbcindonesia.com/market/20251105132236-17-682524/bos-bursa-sebut-duit-danantara-masuk-ke-pasar-modal-tahun-depan)).
- Hire: **Rani Piputri**, ex-Goldman Senior Leader Quant Investment Strategies NY, to head Danantara Public Investment division.
- **State-owned pension / workforce funds** (Taspen, BPJS Ketenagakerjaan etc.) - rising equity asset allocation as additional flow driver (p1).

### 6.5 Danantara - "Value-Up" of Indonesia (p1, p5, p28-29)

**Japan/Korea Value-Up analogy is JPM's explicit framing** - Danantara as Indonesia's Value-Up catalyst.

| Dimension | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Structure** | BPI Danantara (Holding) + **DAM** (Asset Management) + **DIM** (Investment Management) - "proper segregation of duties" separating PSO vs profitability push for SOEs (p1). |
| **Why it matters now** | 2025: US$5bn SOE dividends redirected from MoF → Danantara → constrained fiscal spending + Danantara in setup phase → liquidity parked on balance sheet / in local bonds. **2026 reversal** expected. |
| **Dry powder 2026** | **~US$12bn** = US$5bn (2025 div) + US$5bn (2026E div) + US$3bn Patriot Bonds − US$1.8bn Garuda injection. = **~0.8% of GDP** before multiplier (Figure 54). |
| **Financing flexibility** | Outside 3% fiscal-deficit limit. >**US$14bn commitments** in credit facilities + joint investment funds with global SWFs (QIA etc.) - callable project-by-project, not hard cash (Table 16). Patriot Bond US$3bn hard cash already raised. |
| **9 priority sectors** (Table 15) | Sectors delivering economic/national impact + sustainable returns + scalable ticket for Danantara size. Examples invested/committed: Vale-GEM HPAL, Chandra Asri chemical plant. Full list via Danantara/CNBC/Kompas source. |
| **Market signal** | SOE (ex-banks) basket **+25% YTD vs MXID** on re-rating (Figure 55) - pricing ROE/div improvement; Danantara reform execution in 2026 is "swing factor for valuation re-rating" (p1). |
| **Independence** | Can "generate income, raise external funding, deploy investments, and execute Government spending" independent of fiscal budget - key to plugging soft fiscal capex. |

## 10. Valuation Method - Detailed Logic

JPM's index target is **not a DCF** - it is a **top-down earnings × multiple** framework:

```
YE26 JCI = YE25 JCI × (1 + 2026F market EPS growth) × (Multiple change)
         ≈ 8500 × 1.08 × 1.00 (15x flat) ≈ 9,100
```

- **Earnings assumption:** +8% MSCI Indonesia EPS growth 2026F (consensus contributors per Figure 56 sector contributions). Cross-checked against "negative mid-single digits 2025F" - so swing >10ppt.
- **Multiple assumption:** Flat 15× - explicitly "similar to current levels (assuming no re-rating/de-rating)". Sensitivity is expressed via bull 10,000: implies either ~17x or ~15% EPS surprise.
- **No WACC/beta/ERP disclosure** at index level - those live in single-name DCF/DDM/SOTP models (not in this outlook; stock tables give target-price methodology per analyst, valuations as of 24 Nov 2025).
- **For our report template** this is analogous to the **blended/market-level check** - we should mirror the explicit EPS × multiple bridge + scenario table, and distinguish it from bottom-up DCF/DDM/SOTP that produces per-ticker targets (RATU 7,880 DCF, CDIA 815 DCF/810 DDM, MTEL 630 DCF → 635 blended).

JPM also uses **historical multiple bands** and **yield-spread** as valuation overlays - both are templates we already plan (MTEL PBV/EV/EBITDA bands STD±2).
