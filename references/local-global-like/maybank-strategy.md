## 2. Summary

**Index call:** JCI forward P/E 11.8× (-2 SD vs 10Y avg 15.2×) and LQ45 11.5× (-2 SD) — "highly attractive" but recovery hinges on **USD/IDR reversal**. Twin deficits (fiscal + current account) + high UST 10Y = continued foreign outflow risk. Rate-sensitive names = 28% of JCI (banks 26.2% + property 0.8% + towers 0.9%) and 48.3% of sector weight (IDXFIN 33.3% + IDXPROP 3.2% + IDXINFRA 11.8%) — Fig 2-3.

**Domestic tailwinds (ch. 3):** GDP 5.2% (2025) / 5.3% (2026) from 5.1% 2024E (economist team), BI easing 2025 → domestic demand 2H25, FDI industrial/energy/EV, downstream 26 commodities (Rp9,542T), 3M houses programme (Rp48.5T), NMP spillover.

**Trump 2.0 (ch. 4):** ASEAN not primary target; China+1 benefits FDI to ASEAN; tariff priority is Mexico/Canada/China; Indonesia trade surplus with US only 1% of GDP (vs Thailand 8%, Malaysia 7%) → limited tariff risk; watchlist currency manipulator unlikely.

**Market recap ch. 6–7:** JCI -4.7% YTD / -10.2% 3M, LQ45 -16.3% / -16.2%; foreign flow chapter with equities vs bonds; commodity + sector attribution.

**Coverage universe ch. 9–10:** 35+ stocks, **Fig 62 universe table** (price/TP/upside/EPS growth/PER/ROE/P/B/Div yield FY24E-FY25E) and **Fig 64 sector summary** (sector view POSITIVE/NEUTRAL, top pick, EPS growth, PE, P/BV, div yield).

## 3. Thesis

1. **Mean-reversion in rates → JCI bottom:** UST 10Y elevated due to resilient US economy + Treasury supply + fiscal deficit. Base case: USD weakens in 2025 as Trump opposes excessive dollar strength and deportation/trade policies aren't immediately inflationary → **1Q25 reversal trade in rate-sensitives** (banks, property, towers). Until then, JCI stays sluggish.
2. **Domestic offsets external:** Fiscal impulse (3M houses, VAT stimulus extension, luxury tax DTP for autos/hybrids), downstreaming, and BI easing support earnings even if foreign flows stay negative — **earnings growth 8% YoY is domestic-driven**.
3. **Sector rotation — 3 themes:** (1) Trump-reversal rate-sensitive bounce, (2) Government programme beneficiaries (cement SMGR/INTP, paint AVIA, poultry, dairy/cold chain), (3) Commodity recovery 2Q25 contingent on China stimulus → bottom-fishing coal/nickel/copper.
4. **Stock selection = rate-sensitives + programme leverage + cost leaders:** Banks (BMRI top pick — adequate coverage, loan growth, NIM from CASA), Property (CTRA bellwether + BSDE on footprint), Cement (INTP cost leadership + Grobogan acquisition), Telco/Tower (TOWR/TBIG/MTEL — recurring infra, leverage to consolidation).

## 4. Valuation Method (forensic)

- **Index-level:** `Target = Aggregate core EPS × Fair P/E`. Disclosure: 8% YoY core profit growth → EPS base × 12.9–14.0× = 7,900–8,600. Down from 8,600 (implies prior used ~14× on lower EPS). No DCF, no WACC — **pure multiple**.
  - Analog: **Samuel Strategy Dec2023** uses same method: JCI 7,600 = 14× on EPS 547.4 (FY24) with 5Y avg P/E 22.1 as anchor. Maybank copies this playbook but adds a **range** instead of point estimate (risk-aware).
- **Stock-level (inside strategy):** Each sector p40–70 prints `Price / TP / Upside / PER FY24-25E / P/B FY24-25E / Div Yld FY24-25E` — valuation is **peer multiple + GGM for banks** (P/BV vs ROE), **EV/EBITDA for cement/tower**, **PER for consumer**. No explicit DCF/WACC/g in the strategy PDF — those live in company notes (e.g., BCA BUY 11,675, BRI 5,300, Mandiri 8,000 etc referenced via Fig 1).
- **What this means for template:** Strategy PDF is the **index-level `calc_bands()` + `peers.json` aggregate** — not a single-stock DCF. Our pipeline should treat it as **sector SOTP input**: sum of sector TPs weighted by JCI weight → index target cross-check.
