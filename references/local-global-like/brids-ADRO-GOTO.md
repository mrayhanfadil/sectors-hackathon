## 3. Thesis

**ADRO - spin-off arbitrage:**
1. **Sum > whole** - AADI standalone (coal + 2 GW-adj CFPP) at US$6.1bn > market's implied value inside ADRO at current mkt cap. Spin-off unlocks coal FCF + power asset that were holdco-discounted.
2. **FCF inflection FY26** - coal capex done (US$650mn CFPP remainder in FY25 only), then FCF = EBITDA – minimal capex → dividend upside (>45% payout). Thesis = **yield + FCF**, not growth.
3. **Holdco discount is the risk** - if market assigns bigger discount to remaining ADRO (renewables only, hydro not until FY30, visibility low), ADRO post-spin could trade 31% below current. Renewable pipeline (hydro) at **US$1.1/GW median mkt cap** for global peers is the valuation anchor, but ADRO's  hydropower is 5 years away → discount justified until visibility improves.
4. **Commodity normalization priced** - Newcastle US$120→110/t assumption already bakes in decline; EBITDA still >US$1.1bn - cash generative even at mid-cycle.

**GOTO - tech monetization inflection:**
1. **EBITDA breakeven is the catalyst** - 3Q24 +137bn proves the model; FY24 positive is now base case (was loss-making).
2. **Two growth levers:** ODS (GTV + take rate from ads, 21%+) + GTF (loans +20% qoq, AI-driven underwriting at NPL 1%). Variable cost discipline (+14bps qoq) sustains CM.
3. **Cloud/AI margin unlock** - Alibaba/Tencent contract renewals + LLM for credit scoring → EBITDA margin (% GTV) expansion without GTV acceleration.
4. **Valuation support** - -1 SD gross revenue multiple = mean-reversion entry; DCF fair value 40% above current (64→90).

**BBCA - operational excellence in tight liquidity:** same as Samuel thesis but with **hard numbers**: NIM 6.1→6.3%, CoF 1.1% declining, LDR 78% via loan +14% vs deposit +3%, CoC -0.5% reversal → asset quality allows margin expansion where peers can't.

## 4. Valuation Method (forensic - BEST DISCLOSURE IN SET)

**ADRO = SOTP (explicit, like CDIA):**
- **AADI:** DCF-implied equity **US$6.1bn base case** - inputs: thermal coal operation + CFPP (KPI) valued separately; coal price Newcastle FY25/26F **US$120/110/t**; EBITDA US$1,245/1,119mn; capex US$650mn remainder FY25 → FCF FY26+; dividend >45% payout. Method = **DCF on FCF** for mining + **DCF/asset multiple for CFPP**.
- **ADRO post-spin:** **US$5.3–7.0bn** - range reflects **Holdco discount** assumption (wider discount → lower end). Implied **downside 9–31%** from current mkt cap; AADI subscribers see **+112–171% upside** (US$3.0–4.5bn) if fair value realized. Downside quantified as **US$0.7–2.4bn**.
- **Renewable stub:** Holdco discount minimized if renewable visibility improves; benchmark **US$1.1/GW median mkt cap** for global renewable peers (hydro completion FY30 - far-dated, so not in current SOTP, disclosed as upside option).
- **TP bridge:** **Rp4,100 pre-spin** = weighted SOTP of AADI + remaining ADRO (post-spin values) - no explicit weight disclosed in snapshot, but refer to `Exh. 1-2` (not included in Morning Pack - requires full report).
- **Template mapping:** `calc_sotp([AADI, ADRO_ex_AADI, renewables_stub], holdco_discount)` - exactly CDIA's 4-pilar SOTP pattern (Energy/Water/Port/Logistics → here Coal/Power/Holdco).

**GOTO = DCF (explicit, like MTEL):**
- **Method:** DCF on **GTV-derived revenue** → contribution margin → adj. EBITDA.
- **Key assumption disclosed:** **6% CAGR FY24-34 GTV** for ODS+GTF (conservative - in line with Google-Temasek industry). Take rate 21%+ stable, variable cost stable % GTV, cloud optimization, AI loan growth.
- **Output:** **TP Rp90** (+40.6% from Rp64). FY24-26F forecasts maintained.
- **Cross-check:** **EV/Gross Revenue multiple at -1 SD of 2.5Y mean** - mean-reversion band (like MTEL's PBV/EV bands at ±2 SD). Not blended - single DCF, but multiple is the valuation sanity check (like RATU EV/EBITDA side-by-side).
- **Template mapping:** `calc_dcf(fcf_from_gtv, wacc, g)` + `calc_bands(ev_gross_rev, 2.5Y, -1SD)` - maps to MTEL's `blended 60/40` if we wanted to blend DCF 60% + EV/Gross Rev 40%.

**BBCA Oct24 = Multiple-implied (like Samuel):**
- No DCF in snapshot - TP **Rp12,800** (vs Samuel 9,600 and consensus). Higher TP implies lower CoE or higher g/ROE assumption in BRIDS's GGM. Snapshot only says **Buy TP 12,800** - GGM inputs in full BBCA note.
